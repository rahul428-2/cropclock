Method, data and limits
  • Crop demand. Sentinel-2 L2A over the 8 feeder areas, every pass with <30% cloud (28 dates, Oct 2025 – Mar 2026), cloud-masked with the scene classification layer. Feeder-mean NDVI → crop coefficient, Kc = 1.457·NDVI − 0.1725 (Kamble, Kilic & Hubbard 2013), × FAO-56 reference ET.
  • Weather. Open-Meteo ERA5 archive for observed rain, ET₀ and hourly irradiance. Open-Meteo previous-runs API for the forecasts that were actually issued 1 and 2 days ahead. Rain-skip is scored against what actually fell, so a false alarm costs the crop a day.
  • Feeder. 250 × 7.5 HP pumps on a 50 m borewell (0.341 kWh/m³), 980 kW coincident load, 1 MW MSKVY solar each. Substation cap: 4 feeders at full load. Every feeder keeps its 8 h/day entitlement.
  • Two baselines, both MSEDCL practice. Rota: feeders 1–4 on 08–16 and 5–8 on 22–06, swapping weekly (AgLM Circular 55), still used where the substation can't carry every feeder by day. Daytime-only: 8 h by day, staggered 08–16 / 09–17, the policy for solarised and technically feasible feeders (MSEDCL letter of 31.12.2025). Daytime-only for all 8 feeders needs a 7.84 MW substation, twice the 3.92 MW cap, so it is compared on that bigger substation, against CropClock given the same capacity.
  • Scheduler. Most water-stressed feeder first; each of its hours goes where the day-ahead solar forecast has the most room left after load already placed, never more feeders than the substation carries; once the sun is used up, hours go nearest daylight.
  • Kharif. Monsoon cloud left ~3 usable Sentinel-2 passes, so kharif uses the FAO-56 soybean stage calendar. Its farmer waters defensively: a fixed 60 mm every 12 days (Tier B). Rabi uses an ET-optimal farmer (Tier A, no behavioural assumption).
  • Limits. Feeder command areas are approximated as 500 ha squares: DISCOMs hold the real feeder→field mapping. Outputs are feeder-level aggregates, never plot-level claims.
